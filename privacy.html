Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Animations by Drew Poster ("the app") is a private publishing tool used only by the owner of the Animations by Drew accounts to send videos the owner made to the owner's own TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook accounts.

What the app accesses

  • Basic profile information of the connected account (such as display name), used to confirm the right account is connected.
  • Permission to upload videos to the connected account. On TikTok, videos are sent to the account's inbox as drafts; the account owner reviews and posts each one in the TikTok app.

What the app stores

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used only to upload the owner's videos. The app does not collect, sell or share personal data, does not access other users' data, and does not use any data for advertising.

Removing access

The account owner can revoke access at any time in the connected platform's settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions).
